About this app

Cadence is a calm habit tracker. Plan your day, tick habits off, keep your streaks going, and time the things that take time. No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone.

Habit trackerStreaksPomodoroWidgetsPrivate

Your day at a glance

  • Habits grouped into morning, afternoon, evening and anytime
  • One tap to tick, or count up to a goal like 8 glasses of water
  • A note for any day, and how it went: showed up, good or great

Schedules that fit

  • Days of the week, times per week, days of the month, or every few days
  • Rest days and vacation dates keep your streak safe

Streaks and wins

  • A streak per habit and one for the whole day
  • Three streak freezes a month for the days that get away
  • Celebrations for perfect days, milestones and new bests

Timers built in

  • Stopwatch, countdown or Pomodoro
  • Time goals that tick the habit when you reach them
  • Every session kept in the habit's history

Reminders and widgets

  • Several reminder times per habit, as a notification or an alarm
  • Tick habits and run timers from your home screen

Progress

  • Calendars and completion rates per habit and per day
  • Export a backup file and take it to a new phone

Data safety

No data collectedThe app has no analytics and no account.
No data shared with third partiesThe app doesn't ask for internet access, so it can't send anything.
Everything stays on your phoneBackups are files you save wherever you choose.

Help me launch

  1. Send me the Gmail address you use on your phone.
  2. I'll send you a link to join the test and install Cadence from Play.
  3. Keep it installed for two weeks and use it. Feedback is very welcome.
