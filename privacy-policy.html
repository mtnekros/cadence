Cadence Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Cadence is a habit tracker for Android, made by one independent developer. In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. The app collects nothing, shares nothing, and has no way to send data over the internet.

What the app stores

Your habits, their schedules and reminders, the days you completed them, streaks, notes, logged time and your settings. All of it is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.

What the app collects or shares

Nothing. Cadence has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet at all.

Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings. You choose where it is saved: your phone's storage, a cloud drive app, or anywhere else Android offers. Once saved, that file is handled by whichever app or service you chose, under its own terms. Importing a backup only reads the file you pick.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications: habit reminders and the timer.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): reminders at the exact time you set.
  • Full-screen notifications: reminders and timers you set to ring as an alarm can show over the lock screen.
  • Run at startup: puts your reminders back after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration and keeping the device awake: so a reminder or timer alarm is noticed.
  • Read storage (Android 12 and older only): to open a backup file you choose to import.

None of these permissions are used to collect or send information.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, and everything it stored is deleted from your device. Backup files you exported are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

Children

Cadence is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
